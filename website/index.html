Features

Event-Driven World

Everything is an event. Time, weather, astronomy, system state — all flow through a central event bus that brings the world to life.

Day/Night Cycle

The world changes with real local time. Dawn breaks, stars emerge, and the town settles into its nocturnal rhythm.

Anomaly System

Rare, strange events that feel intentional. A second moon. A watcher in the pines. A signal from the observatory.

Journal & Secrets

Discoveries persist across sessions. The world remembers what you've found — and what you haven't.

Multi-Monitor

Independent or synchronized worlds per display. Different seeds, different experiences.

Privacy First

No telemetry. No accounts. No tracking. The weirdness comes from simulation, not surveillance.

The Town That Wasn't There

A cozy mysterious town with pine forest, distant mountains, an old radio tower, a small cabin, and an abandoned observatory. Strange lights. Handwritten notes. A mysterious moon.

The world should feel cozy before it feels frightening. Cute + mysterious + weird + nostalgic + funny + occasionally unsettling.
